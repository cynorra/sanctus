Saint of the Day

Saint Benedict of Nursia

Abbot, Patriarch of Western Monks · c. 480 – 548

Saint Benedict founded the great monastery of Monte Cassino and wrote the famous Rule of Saint Benedict. His motto, “Ora et Labora” (Pray and Work), balances contemplation with diligent sacred service.

Saint Benedict Medal Inscription:

“Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux! Non Draco Sit Mihi Dux!” (May the Holy Cross be my light! Let not the dragon be my guide!)
