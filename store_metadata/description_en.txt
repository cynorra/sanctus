Sanctus is the most complete Christian prayer companion — beautiful, offline-first, and available in 35 languages for Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant believers worldwide.

**CANONICAL HOURS — LITURGY OF THE HOURS**
Pray with the Church throughout the day. Sanctus guides you through all six canonical hours:
• Lauds (Morning Prayer) — 6:00 AM
• Terce (Mid-Morning) — 9:00 AM
• Sext (Midday Prayer) — 12:00 PM
• None (Mid-Afternoon) — 3:00 PM
• Vespers (Evening Prayer) — 6:00 PM
• Compline (Night Prayer) — 9:00 PM

Smart notifications remind you at each canonical hour, even after restarting your phone.

**HOLY ROSARY**
A guided Rosary with automatic mystery selection by day of the week — Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, and Luminous Mysteries. Track each bead, decade, and mystery with a single tap.

**SAINT OF THE DAY**
Discover a new saint every day — 30 saints with full biographies, feast dates, and patronages. From Saint Francis of Assisi to Saint Teresa of Calcutta.

**LITURGICAL CALENDAR**
Follow the Church year through Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, and Ordinary Time. 72 solemnities, feasts, and memorials with full descriptions. Never miss a Holy Day of Obligation.

**PRAYER LIBRARY**
Over 110 authentic prayers in 33 languages — Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, Morning Offering, Vespers, Compline, Memorare, Anima Christi, Prayer to Saint Michael, and more. Adjustable font size for comfortable reading.
